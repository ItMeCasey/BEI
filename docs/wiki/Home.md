# Barely Enough Items (BEI)

A minimalist item and recipe viewer for Minecraft. The item browser sits against the right edge of inventory screens, recipes open in a slim popup grouped by station, and the Master Blueprint tracker breaks any item into a nested crafting plan pinned to your HUD.

**Versions:** Forge 26.1.2 and Forge 1.21.1.
