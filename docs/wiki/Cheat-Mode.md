# Cheat Mode

**Ctrl+click the gear** to turn cheat mode on or off (the gear goes red). It lasts for the session.

Who can use it: creative players, operators (game master permission), and you alone in your own singleplayer world. On a server, BEI must be installed on the server too, and the server checks permission on every request. No commands are used.

Set **Cheat items in** in the settings:

- **Cursor**: left-click an item in the browser to *hold* it. BEI remembers the item and count and draws them just below the mouse pointer. Each further click adds one; **Shift+click** sets a full stack; clicking a different item swaps it. Click a slot to place the items there (left click all, right click one). **Esc**, clicking outside any slot or closing the screen drops what you hold without creating anything.
- **Inventory**: each click puts the item straight into your inventory (Shift for a full stack).

Nothing is ever spawned into the world.
